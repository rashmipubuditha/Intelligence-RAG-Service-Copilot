## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the requirements for Customer Due Diligence (CDD) conducted by the platform on behalf of regulated firms. It defines the tiers of CDD, the circumstances under which each applies, and the minimum evidence standards required at each tier.

This is the **previous version** of the CDD Policy. It is included in this repository only as a fixture: it contradicts the current v2.1 in two places so that the system's conflict-detection behaviour can be demonstrated and tested.

## 5. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

EDD is mandatory for any client who:

- Scores above 75 on the Risk Assessment, regardless of other factors
- Is identified as a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) at the foreign tier only
- Has a confirmed nexus to a sanctioned country, entity, or individual

EDD requirements are in addition to Standard CDD and include:

- Enhanced Source of Wealth verification with a minimum of one corroborating source
- Senior management approval before the business relationship is established or continued
- Annual periodic review

---

## 7. Ongoing Monitoring

All client relationships are subject to ongoing monitoring. The frequency of periodic reviews is determined by the assigned CDD tier and risk band:

| Risk Band | CDD Tier | Review Frequency |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Low (0–30) | SDD | Every 36 months |
| Medium (31–60) | Standard CDD | Every 24 months |
| High (61–100) | EDD | Every 12 months |

Reviews must be triggered immediately (outside the scheduled cycle) upon a material change in the client's circumstances or a sanctions alert.

---

## 8. Record Keeping

All CDD records must be retained for a minimum of **three years** from the end of the business relationship or the date of the last transaction, whichever is later.
