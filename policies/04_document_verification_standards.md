# Document Verification Standards
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Owner:** MLRO  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This document sets the minimum standards for the verification of identity and address documents submitted by clients during onboarding and periodic review. It defines which documents are acceptable, their age requirements, the verification process for remote and in-person channels, and the rules governing certified copies and translations.

Document verification is a control, not a formality. The reviewing officer is responsible for the quality and completeness of the documents they accept.

---

## 2. Acceptable Primary Identity Documents

A primary identity document must be government-issued, photographic, and contain the holder's full legal name and date of birth. The following are accepted:

| Document Type | Jurisdiction | Conditions |
|---------------|-------------|------------|
| Passport | Any | Valid, **or** expired within the last **6 months** |
| National Identity Card | EU/EEA countries and UK | Must be valid at the time of submission |
| Biometric Residence Permit | UK | Must be valid at the time of submission |
| Driving Licence (photocard) | UK, EU/EEA, US, Canada, Australia | Must be valid; paper licences not accepted |

**A document issued more than 10 years ago is not acceptable** as a primary identity document, even if it technically remains valid (this most commonly affects older passports with 10-year validity that are still in date but were issued more than 10 years ago).

Only one primary identity document is required. If the document is damaged, partially illegible, or of poor quality, the client must be asked to resubmit with a clearer copy or an alternative document.

---

## 3. Acceptable Proof of Address Documents

A proof of address document must show the client's full name and current residential address. It must have been **issued within the last 3 months** at the time of submission.

The following are accepted:

- Bank or building society statement (original or electronic, from a regulated institution)
- Utility bill (gas, electricity, water, broadband — mobile phone bills are not accepted)
- Council tax bill (current year)
- HMRC correspondence (e.g., tax return notice, NI correspondence)
- Mortgage statement (from a regulated lender)
- Tenancy agreement (signed and dated, covering the current period)

A **mobile phone bill is not an acceptable proof of address** under any circumstances.

The proof of address must match the residential address declared by the client. Where there is a discrepancy, the reviewing officer must request an explanation and document it on the file.

---

## 4. Document Age and Validity Rules

The following rules apply regardless of document type:

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Primary ID validity | Passport: valid or expired within 6 months. National ID / BRP / Driving Licence: must be valid at submission |
| Primary ID issuance age | Must have been issued within the last 10 years |
| Proof of address age | Must have been issued within the last 3 months |
| Supporting SoW documents | Age requirements vary by document type — see Source of Wealth Verification Guidelines (Policy 02) |

Where a client is unable to provide documents meeting these requirements, the reviewing officer must escalate to the MLRO before proceeding.

---

## 5. Remote Onboarding and Biometric Verification

All clients onboarding through the platform's remote digital channel are subject to biometric identity verification. This requirement cannot be waived for remote clients.

Biometric verification must:

- Capture a live image of the client's face (liveness check)
- Extract and compare the facial biometric against the primary identity document submitted
- Return a confidence score from the verification provider
- Be conducted through a provider approved by the MLRO (current approved providers are listed in the Platform Technology Register)

Where biometric verification fails or returns a confidence score below the approved threshold, the client must:

1. Be invited to retry (up to two additional attempts)
2. Be escalated to the MLRO if all attempts fail

A client may not be onboarded remotely without a passed biometric verification check. There is no manual override at the reviewing officer level; only the MLRO may approve an exception and must document the basis for doing so.

---

## 6. Certified Copies

Where a client submits physical documents (rather than digital originals), certified copies are required. A certified copy must be:

- A clear photocopy of the original document
- Certified by an approved certifier (see below) with a statement that it is a true copy of the original
- Dated within the last **3 months**
- Signed with the certifier's name printed, their profession, and contact details

**Approved certifiers** include:

- Solicitor or legal professional regulated in the UK or equivalent jurisdiction
- Chartered accountant or chartered certified accountant
- Bank or building society official
- Doctor, nurse, or other regulated healthcare professional
- Police officer
- Teacher or headteacher at a state school
- Notary public

A friend, family member, or employer (unless the employer is a regulated firm) is not an approved certifier.

---

## 7. Document Translation

Where a document is in a language other than English, a certified translation is required. The translation must be:

- Produced by a professional translator (not the client or a family member)
- Accompanied by the translator's credentials and a statement of accuracy
- Submitted alongside the original-language document

Machine translation (e.g., Google Translate) is not acceptable. AI-generated translations are not acceptable without human certification.

---

## 8. Document Quality Standards

Documents submitted digitally must meet the following quality standards:

- Minimum resolution: 300 DPI
- All four corners of the document must be visible
- No glare, obscuring reflections, or fingers covering content
- The image must be in colour; black-and-white scans are not accepted for primary identity documents
- File format: PDF, JPEG, or PNG

Where a submitted document fails quality standards, the client must be asked to resubmit. A document that does not meet quality standards must not be accepted or marked as verified.

---

## 9. Record Keeping

The original (or a faithful digital copy of the original) of every verified document must be stored on the client file. The record must include:

- Document type and issuing authority
- Date of issue and expiry (where applicable)
- Date received and date verified
- Name of the reviewing officer
- Verification outcome (Accepted / Rejected / Escalated)
- For biometric checks: confidence score and provider reference number

Records must be retained for five years from the end of the business relationship.
