# Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Policy
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Owner:** MLRO  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the requirements for Customer Due Diligence (CDD) conducted by the platform on behalf of regulated firms. It defines the three tiers of CDD, the circumstances under which each applies, and the minimum evidence standards required at each tier.

All CDD must be completed before a business relationship is established or, where permitted, as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter. No client file may be marked complete without a documented CDD outcome.

---

## 2. CDD Tiers

The platform operates three tiers of due diligence:

| Tier | Name | Trigger |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Simplified Due Diligence (SDD) | Low-risk clients meeting all SDD eligibility criteria |
| 2 | Standard Due Diligence (Standard CDD) | Default tier for all clients not qualifying for SDD or requiring EDD |
| 3 | Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) | High-risk clients, all PEPs, clients with nexus to sanctioned jurisdictions |

Tier assignment is determined by the Risk Assessment Framework (see Policy 05). Once assigned, the tier governs the minimum evidence, verification steps, and review frequency applicable to the client.

---

## 3. Simplified Due Diligence (SDD)

SDD may only be applied where **all** of the following conditions are met:

- The client is a UK-regulated financial institution or a listed company on a recognised exchange.
- The client's beneficial ownership structure is transparent and publicly verifiable.
- The client's risk score is 30 or below (Low risk band).
- No adverse media, sanctions, or PEP flags have been identified.

Under SDD, the firm must still obtain and record the client's name, registered address, and company registration number. Identity verification steps may be abbreviated but not omitted entirely.

SDD eligibility must be reassessed at every periodic review and immediately upon any material change to the client relationship.

---

## 4. Standard CDD

Standard CDD applies to all individual and entity clients who do not qualify for SDD and do not require EDD.

### 4.1 Individual Clients

The following must be obtained and verified for all individual clients:

- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Residential address (current)
- Nationality and country of birth
- One primary photographic identity document (see Document Verification Standards, Policy 04)
- One proof of address document dated within three months
- Source of Wealth declaration and supporting evidence (see Policy 02)

### 4.2 Entity Clients

For corporate entities, partnerships, trusts and other legal arrangements, the firm must obtain:

- Registered name and trading name (if different)
- Registered address and principal place of business
- Company registration number and jurisdiction of incorporation
- Nature of business and purpose of the relationship
- Beneficial ownership structure to the level of all individuals holding 25% or more ownership or control
- Identity verification for each beneficial owner meeting the 25% threshold, to the same standard as individual clients
- Source of Funds and Source of Wealth for the entity and its principal beneficial owners

For complex ownership structures (e.g., layered holding companies, nominee shareholders), all intermediate layers must be documented and verified. A simplified structure chart must be included in the client file.

---

## 5. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

EDD is mandatory for any client who:

- Scores above 60 on the Risk Assessment (High risk band), regardless of other factors
- Is identified as a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) at any tier, regardless of risk score
- Has a confirmed nexus to a sanctioned country, entity, or individual
- Is a cash-intensive business (e.g., retail, hospitality, money services)
- Has a complex or opaque beneficial ownership structure that cannot be fully verified

EDD requirements are in addition to Standard CDD and include:

- Enhanced Source of Wealth verification with a minimum of two independent corroborating sources
- Senior management approval before the business relationship is established or continued
- Documented rationale for why the relationship is appropriate despite elevated risk
- Six-monthly periodic review (as opposed to annual for Standard CDD clients)
- Ongoing transaction monitoring at an elevated threshold

If EDD cannot be satisfactorily completed, the relationship must not be established or must be exited. The MLRO must be notified in all cases where EDD cannot be completed.

---

## 6. Timing of CDD

CDD must be completed **before** the business relationship commences. The only permitted exception is where:

- Delaying would interrupt normal business and the risk of money laundering is low; **and**
- CDD is completed as soon as reasonably practicable after initial contact; **and**
- The MLRO has been notified and has approved the exception in writing.

No exception may be applied to EDD clients. EDD must always be completed before the relationship begins.

---

## 7. Ongoing Monitoring

All client relationships are subject to ongoing monitoring. The frequency of periodic reviews is determined by the assigned CDD tier and risk band:

| Risk Band | CDD Tier | Review Frequency |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Low (0–30) | SDD | Every 24 months |
| Medium (31–60) | Standard CDD | Every 12 months |
| High (61–100) | EDD | Every 6 months |

Reviews must be triggered immediately (outside the scheduled cycle) upon:

- A material change in the client's circumstances (change of address, business activity, beneficial ownership)
- A sanctions or adverse media alert
- An unusual or unexplained transaction pattern
- A request from the MLRO or a regulatory authority

---

## 8. Record Keeping

All CDD records — documents, verification outcomes, risk scores, and review notes — must be retained for a minimum of **five years** from the end of the business relationship or the date of the last transaction, whichever is later.

Records must be stored in a manner that is tamper-evident and readily retrievable for regulatory inspection within 48 hours of a request.

---

## 9. Responsibility

The MLRO is responsible for maintaining this policy and approving any exceptions. Regulated firms using the platform are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information they provide. The platform is responsible for ensuring that its workflows enforce these requirements and that no client file can be submitted for sign-off without a complete and documented CDD outcome.
