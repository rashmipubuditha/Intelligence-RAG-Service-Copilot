# Sanctions and PEP Screening Procedure
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Owner:** MLRO  
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how the platform screens clients and their associated parties against sanctions lists and Politically Exposed Person (PEP) databases. It covers initial screening at onboarding, ongoing periodic screening, the process for managing alerts, and the escalation and notification obligations that apply when a match is confirmed.

Failure to screen, or screening without appropriate follow-up, is a regulatory breach. This procedure must be followed without exception.

---

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Sanctions

A sanction is a legal prohibition or restriction imposed by a government or international body against a person, entity, or jurisdiction. The platform screens against:

- UK HM Treasury Consolidated Sanctions List (UK)
- OFAC Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List (US)
- EU Consolidated Sanctions List
- UN Security Council Consolidated List

Screening must be conducted against all active lists simultaneously. A client is considered sanctioned if they appear on any list, regardless of which jurisdiction imposed the measure.

### 2.2 Politically Exposed Person (PEP)

A Politically Exposed Person is an individual who holds, or has held within the last **12 months**, a prominent public function. The platform recognises three PEP tiers:

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Domestic PEP | Holds or recently held a prominent public function in the UK (e.g., senior government minister, MP, senior judicial officer, senior military officer, board member of a state-owned enterprise) |
| Foreign PEP | Holds or recently held a prominent public function in a country other than the UK |
| International Organisation PEP | Holds or recently held a senior position in an international organisation (e.g., UN, IMF, EU institutions, World Bank) |

**Close associates and family members of PEPs** are subject to the same screening and enhanced diligence requirements as the PEP themselves. Close associates include: spouse or civil partner, children and their spouses, parents, and known business partners.

A PEP who left public office more than **12 months ago** transitions to standard screening unless the MLRO determines residual risk warrants continued enhanced treatment.

---

## 3. Initial Screening at Onboarding

Every client, beneficial owner, director, and authorised signatory must be screened before the business relationship is established. Screening must be completed against:

1. All sanctions lists defined in Section 2.1
2. The global PEP database
3. Adverse media database (minimum 12-month lookback)

Screening must be documented with:

- The date and time of screening
- The screening provider and list versions used
- The result (No Match / Potential Match / Confirmed Match)
- Where a potential match is identified: the match details and the reviewing officer's disposition

No client file may proceed to the CDD completion stage until screening is documented as either No Match or a confirmed Potential Match that has been reviewed and dispositioned.

---

## 4. Ongoing Periodic Screening

Screening is not a one-time event. All active client relationships are subject to ongoing screening at intervals determined by the client's risk band:

| Risk Band | Screening Frequency |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Low (0–30) | Annually |
| Medium (31–60) | Quarterly |
| High (61–100) | Monthly |

In addition to scheduled periodic screening, the platform operates **continuous monitoring** for sanctions list updates. Any addition or amendment to a sanctions list triggers an immediate re-screen of all active clients within **24 hours** of the list update being published.

PEP database updates trigger an immediate re-screen of all active clients within **48 hours**.

## 5. Alert Management

### 5.1 False Positives

A potential match is a false positive where:

- The name match is coincidental and the client's date of birth, nationality, or other attributes clearly distinguish them from the listed individual or entity; **and**
- The reviewing officer has documented the basis for the false positive determination with specificity

A false positive may be dispositioned by a trained compliance officer without MLRO approval, provided:

- The match score is below the provider's high-confidence threshold; **and**
- The disposition is documented with the officer's name, date, and written reasoning

### 5.2 Potential Matches Requiring MLRO Review

A potential match must be escalated to the MLRO where:

- The match score meets or exceeds the provider's high-confidence threshold
- There is any overlap in nationality, date of birth, or known aliases in addition to the name match
- The reviewing officer is uncertain and cannot confidently rule out the match

The MLRO must review and provide a documented determination within **48 hours** of escalation.

### 5.3 Confirmed True Matches

Where a match is confirmed as a true match (the client is, or is associated with, a sanctioned individual or entity):

- The business relationship must be **immediately suspended** pending further review
- The MLRO must be notified **within 24 hours** of the confirmed match
- The platform's legal counsel must be engaged
- A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed with the National Crime Agency (NCA) where required by law
- No funds or assets may be moved without explicit MLRO and legal sign-off

Documentation of all steps taken must be created contemporaneously and stored on the client file. Records must not be altered after the fact.

---

## 6. PEP-Specific Requirements

Where a client is identified as a PEP (at any tier), regardless of the outcome of sanctions screening:

- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is automatically triggered (see CDD Policy, Section 5)
- Senior management approval is required before the relationship is established or continued
- The basis for proceeding must be documented and signed off by a senior manager
- The relationship is subject to six-monthly review as a minimum
- Source of Wealth verification must meet the two-source corroboration standard applicable to EDD clients

A PEP status alone does not prohibit a relationship from proceeding. However, the bar for documentation, approval, and ongoing monitoring is materially higher than for non-PEP clients.

---

## 7. Record Keeping

All screening records must be retained for a minimum of five years from the end of the business relationship. Records must include:

- Screening date, provider, and list versions
- All alerts generated, including potential and false positives
- Disposition of every alert, with officer name, date, and written rationale
- MLRO notifications and responses
- SAR filings and internal records (subject to tipping-off restrictions)
